Self-Assessment Exam Simulator (Windows)
========================================

WHAT IT DOES
Take self-assessment PDF forms like a real exam: timed blocks,
highlighting, strike-through, lab values, calculator - then get scored
against the answer key with full explanations.

HOW TO START (first time only)
1. Put "Exam Simulator.exe" anywhere you like (Desktop is fine).
2. Double-click it. Windows will likely show a blue warning saying
   "Windows protected your PC" - this is normal for any app that isn't
   from a big company. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   (This warning only appears the first time.)
3. Your browser opens automatically after a few seconds.

EVERY TIME AFTER THAT
1. Double-click "Exam Simulator.exe" and wait a few seconds.
2. Upload the two PDFs for a form:
     - the "Questions" PDF
     - the matching "Answer Key" PDF
3. Set your time limit and start.

TIPS
- Select text with your mouse to highlight it (click a highlight to remove it).
- Click an answer to choose it. Right-click an answer to cross it out.
- Press M to mark a question, arrow keys to navigate, A-E to answer.
- Your progress saves automatically - if you close the tab and come back
  (same computer, same browser), your answers are still there.
- To quit, close the browser tab. The app stops when you restart your
  computer, or end "Exam Simulator" from Task Manager.

REQUIREMENTS
- Windows 10 or 11.
- Works best in Chrome, but any browser is fine.
